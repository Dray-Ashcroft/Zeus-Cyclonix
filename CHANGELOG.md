## RC X · Vegetation

*Current release*

**Added**
- Maize fields, grass tufts, clover and wildflowers on the plains
- Tree groves and ferns along the lake and rivers
- Forest on the lower slopes, thinning towards the tree line
- Rain ripples on water, sea mist near saturation, damp river banks and boulders on bare slopes
- Soft sun shadows from trees, crops and mountains

**Changed**
- Plants sway with the Wind slider
- Sharper plant textures and ground at low viewing angles
- Sun glows with a soft corona and fine light streaks; glow, streaks and light shafts scale with the Sun's Heat slider

---

## RC IX · Molecules

**Added**
- 3D water molecules inside the cloud magnifier
- Liquid-water structure inside a cloud droplet
- Freezing cloud: hexagonal ice lattice and snowflake growth

---

## RC VIII · Refinement

**Added**
- Tap the sky or press Space to pause
- Automatic quality reduction on slower panels
- Project Designer credit

**Changed**
- Minimal icon control bar; Controls and Data tabs shown as icons
- Single loading screen
- Project split into `index.html`, `style.css` and `script.js`
- Groundwater flows naturally on every cut-away face

**Removed**
- Explain panel and Quick Check

**Fixed**
- Leftover magma glow on all rock faces

---

## RC VII · Water and Precipitation

**Added**
- Flowing water surfaces for the river and groundwater
- Snowflakes fall from the cloud to the peaks

**Changed**
- Rain drawn as natural streaks
- Water vapour drawn as soft mist

---

## RC VI · 3D Models

## RC I · Initial Release

**Added**
- 3D water cycle: evaporation, condensation and precipitation

- Dew point, cloud base and water budget readouts
